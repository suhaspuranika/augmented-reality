Why isn't my printer detected?

Web Bluetooth (navigator.bluetooth) can only talk to Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE / GATT) devices. Most thermal / POS printers use Bluetooth Classic (BR/EDR) for print jobs, which the browser cannot connect to at all.

  • If your printer shows BR/EDR (no LE), Web Bluetooth cannot use it.
  • Classic printers appear as "Unknown or Unsupported Device" in the browser picker.
  • Windows has the weakest Web Bluetooth support; Chrome/Edge on Android or ChromeOS work best.

Ways that do work with a Classic (BR/EDR) printer:

  • Print via System Dialog (button above) after pairing the printer in your OS and installing its driver.
  • A network/Wi-Fi printer variant (send ESC/POS to its IP from a backend).
  • A small local bridge app on the paired machine that your web app POSTs to.
